Questions

Good to know.

Which skin types is this for?

The Everyday Collection is made for most skin types, including sensitive skin. Patch test any new product first.

How long until I notice a difference?

Most people feel more comfortable skin within one to two weeks. Give a new routine at least four weeks before judging it.

What does shipping cost?

Shipping is free on orders over $75. Below that, a flat rate is shown at checkout.

Can I return a product?

Unopened products can be returned within 30 days. Opened products can be exchanged if they don’t suit your skin.

Is this a real store?

No. This is a concept site, so the products are placeholders and checkout is switched off.
